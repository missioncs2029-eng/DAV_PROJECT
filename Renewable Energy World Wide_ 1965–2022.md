# Renewable Energy World Wide: 1965–2022

## Project Overview

This project analyzes the growth, trends, and global development of renewable energy from **1965 to 2022**, using historical energy data and visualization techniques.

* **Core Question:** How has renewable energy production and consumption evolved worldwide from 1965 to 2022?
* **Objective:** Perform exploratory data analysis and create meaningful visualizations to understand global renewable energy trends, growth patterns, and the contribution of different renewable energy sources over time.
